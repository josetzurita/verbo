1. Executive Summary

Findings combine technical, content, schema, performance, visual, and search-readiness evidence as available.

2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 82/100

What Works

  • http and www 301 to https apex
  • All 12 sitemap URLs 200, self-canonical, indexable
  • Real 404 with noindex for unknown URLs
  • Content fully in raw HTML
  • llms.txt present

Findings

No security headers High

No CSP, X-Content-Type-Options, frame protection, Referrer-Policy or Permissions-Policy on any response.

Recommendation: Add public/_headers with nosniff, frame protection, Referrer-Policy strict-origin-when-cross-origin and Permissions-Policy. HSTS later.

Hashed assets not cached High

/_next/static/* and images return Cache-Control: public, max-age=0, must-revalidate.

Recommendation: _headers: /_next/static/* max-age=31536000, immutable; image folders a long max-age.

/styleguide/ is a soft 404 Medium

Returns 200 with a 'Page not found' body and noindex.

Recommendation: Remove out/styleguide in the production build so the 404 page handles it.

Duplicate robots meta on 404 Medium

404 page has both 'noindex' and 'noindex, nofollow', and og:url points to the home page.

Recommendation: Emit one robots tag and drop og:url on the 404.

Small leftovers Low

307 for slash-less URLs (Workers), og:type website on cases, deprecated Host: line in robots.txt, no IndexNow.

Recommendation: Optional: og:type article for cases, drop Host:, add an IndexNow key.

2.2 Content Quality

Score: 52/100

What Works

  • Eight case bodies are genuinely distinct (max 1.8% overlap)
  • Real named clients, six link to live sites
  • Service passages are self-contained definitions
  • Readability fits a business audience

Findings

Placeholder team Critical

'Juliana Cabrera' appears five times with roles Strategy/Design/Research/Copy/Direction (lib/home-content.ts).

Recommendation: Show the real team, or one founder card for Juliana Cabrera Bonilla with a short bio and Person schema.

No proof in case studies High

No year, client quote or measurable outcome on any case.

Recommendation: Add a facts row (year, client, country, scope) and one client quote or result per case.

No service pages High

Naming, strategy and rebranding queries have no landing page. Verbo created the Albora, Altalba, Lienzo, Autip and Lukatto names.

Recommendation: Start with a Naming page, then Brand strategy and Real-estate branding.

No outside authority signals High

No awards, press, directories or social profiles.

Recommendation: Create LinkedIn, Google Business Profile, Behance and Clutch/Sortlist profiles.

Home copy rendered twice Medium

Desktop and mobile versions of projects and services are both in the HTML: 2,217 words, about 1,100 unique.

Recommendation: Render one version and adapt it with CSS where possible.

2.3 On-Page SEO

Score: 63/100

What Works

  • Unique titles and descriptions on all pages
  • One H1 per page
  • Every page reachable from internal links

Findings

Headings lose word spaces High

Raw HTML: H1 'Creatingtoshapetheworld.', H2 'Averbdoesnotdescribe.Itacts.', footer H2 'yoursdeserves' on every page.

Recommendation: Insert a space text node between word spans in the split-text components.

Home H1 contains the CTA and no topic term High

H1 text includes 'Schedule a free discovery session'; no 'branding' or 'Bogotá'.

Recommendation: Move the CTA out of the H1.

Case and /work/ titles carry no search terms Medium

'Work - Verbo', 'Albora, case study - Verbo'.

Recommendation: For example 'Branding projects in Colombia - Verbo', 'Albora - residential brand naming and identity - Verbo'.

Case H1 is a slogan Medium

For example 'From the field to your table' instead of 'Berry House'.

Recommendation: Keep the slogan visually but make the project name part of the H1.

2.5 Performance (CWV)

Score: 68/100

What Works

  • CLS 0 everywhere
  • Fonts self-hosted via next/font
  • Case pages serve AVIF srcset with blur placeholders
  • GA4 not loaded before consent

Findings

Intro loader blocks LCP High

Lab: home LCP 20.9s mobile / 5.3s desktop; the loader decodes 8 untransformed 2500px Sanity JPEGs (about 3.1 MB).

Recommendation: Request them at 800-1200w with auto=format, preload only the first, and gate the loader on the first frame.

Close-section plates are heavy Medium

About 2.3 MB on desktop for plates shown at 160-220px.

Recommendation: Cap generated widths near 480px.

Case LCP tied to the reveal animation Low

Mobile 4.6s with 1.7s render delay; the hero AVIF is only 75 KB.

Recommendation: Make the first case image eager and high priority, with no opacity-0 start.

2.6 AI Search Readiness

Score: 55/100

What Works

  • All AI crawlers get 200 and server-rendered HTML
  • llms.txt valid
  • Agent-friendliness check 91/100; the menu dialog is accessible

Findings

robots.txt vs terms on AI training Medium

The terms forbid AI training; robots.txt allows GPTBot, CCBot, Google-Extended and others.

Recommendation: Owner decision: block training crawlers only and add Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=no, or soften the terms.

CTA links nameless until scrolled Medium

underline-link.tsx hides labels with autoAlpha 0, so 'Get to know us' and 'Meet the team' have no accessible name at load.

Recommendation: Animate opacity only (no visibility), or add an aria-label.

Brand name inconsistency Low

'Verbo' in titles and schema, 'Verbo Brands' in copy; 'Verbo' collides with the Spanish word.

Recommendation: Use 'Verbo Brands' as alternateName and in key copy.

2.7 Images

Score: 52/100

What Works

  • Width and height set, no layout shift

Findings

Oversized PNGs in public/ High

about/portrait.png is 795 KB at 605x908; belief/sequence-1/2/3.png are 143-280 KB and preloaded though below the fold.

Recommendation: Convert to WebP/AVIF and remove the preloads.
